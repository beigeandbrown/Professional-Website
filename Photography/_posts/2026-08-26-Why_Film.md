&emsp;I began my photography journey during the holidays about 2 years ago after finding my mother's old EOS Rebel Ti in a cabinet. After getting some batteries and testing it, I decided to research more into how I would go about taking some pictures, of course, starting with buying film.


&emsp;I was intrigued by the aesthetics of Wes Anderson films and after a quick search, I decided that Ektar 100 was the film stock I needed to take pictures that look straight out of Grand Budapest Hotel (I was wrong).
