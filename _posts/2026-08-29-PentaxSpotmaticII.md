&emsp;The next camera I got was a hand me down from my grandfather. The Pentax Spotmatic II. Released in 1971, this camera was most famous for its super-multicoated takumar lenses that were fast, inexpensive, well built, and sharp. It is the quintessential vintage manual slr camera. Shooting this was a really nice contrast from my autofocus elan 7, getting to manually set apertures, shutter speeds, and focus distance before pressing the nice spring loaded release and winding forward to the next frame. Unfortunately the light meter was broken as are many of the selenium based vintage meters. To account for this, I use a Reveni Labs light meter in aperture priority mode.
